Quorum keeps every Company in this browser. To move one, copy its save text from Settings and
import that text in the other browser.

## Copy a Company

1. Open the Company on the Board.
2. Open **Settings** and select **Copy save as text**.
3. Keep the text. A note file or a message to yourself is enough.

The text holds the Company's Design, Cash, Insight, Research, Team, Users, clock, Dashboard
history, Inbox, and the parts of the Board it has revealed.

## Import a Company

1. Open the Company that should receive the save. Start a Company if the other browser has none.
2. Open **Settings** and select **Import a save**.
3. Paste the text and confirm.

Import replaces the open Company's Board with the one in the text. Copy the open Company first if
you want to keep both.

The save text carries a format version. A save from a different format is refused with a message,
and the open Company stays as it was.

## What stays in this browser

Quorum has no account. The browser holds the Company files, the list of Companies, Settings, the
Founder record, Sandbox progress, and the support log. It also holds the Agent Room key for a
Company with Agent access on, and the display name and Seat used by online Rooms.

Clearing site data deletes all of it, including every Company. Safari can remove site storage after
seven days without a visit, so copy a Company you care about before you stop playing for a while.

The [privacy page](/about/privacy) says what the game sends to its analytics service. None of it
identifies a player.
