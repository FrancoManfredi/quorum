In this lesson you end your first Company with an Exit, earn Legacy, and start a new Company. Your
first Exit becomes available in **Series A**, so this lesson starts in Pre-seed and spans a long
session.

## Reach Series A

Each funding round opens when your users reach its target. You raised the first round in
[Your first incident](/tutorials/first-incident). Raise two more the same way.

1. Grow to the Pre-seed target. The **Seed offer** button appears under the Era name on the HUD.
2. Click it, and select **Raise**. Read the Era card, and select **Continue**.
3. Grow to the Seed target. The **Series A offer** button appears.
4. Click it, and select **Raise**. Read the Era card, and select **Continue**.

The [Eras](/eras) reference lists each target. Each round gives Cash and takes a share of your
Equity. Keep your Equity in mind: your Exit pays only your share.

On the way, use what you learned. Fix each bottleneck, respond to each incident, and file each
postmortem. [Find the bottleneck](/guides/find-the-bottleneck) is a good companion.

## Find the Exit button

When the HUD shows **Series A**, a new **Exit** button appears in the toolbar at the top right. It
has a door icon. A green badge on it shows the most Legacy you can earn now. On a phone, Exit is
in the **More** menu.

While the badge is missing, your Exit would pay no Legacy. Keep growing until the badge shows a
number.

:::Note{tone="real"}
An acqui-hire is a real kind of exit: a larger company buys a startup mainly to hire its team. It
pays less than a full acquisition, and founders often take it to start again quickly.
:::

## Read the offer

1. Click **Exit**. The Exit panel opens.
2. Read the four numbers at the top:
   - **Valuation**: what the market thinks your Company is worth now.
   - **Rule of 40**: your user growth plus your margin. A higher score raises the valuation.
   - **Equity**: the share of the Company that you still own.
   - **Legacy ×**: the multiplier from your Venture and Mutators.
3. Read the **Acqui-hire** row. It shows the payout and the Exit's multiplier.

The payout is your Equity times the valuation times the Exit's multiplier. An Acqui-hire pays on a
capped valuation at half the multiplier. Legacy grows with the square root of the payout, so a
payout four times as large earns twice the Legacy.

From **Series B**, an **Acquisition** row appears too. It pays the full valuation. You can wait for
it, but this lesson takes the first Exit, to show you the whole loop.

## Take the Exit

1. Select **Take** on the **Acqui-hire** row. The button shows the Legacy it pays.
2. Read the summary. It shows a small drawing of your final Design and your Legacy, such as
   **+3 Legacy**.
3. Read the four numbers under it: **Payout**, **Peak users**, **Era**, and **Played**.
4. If you want, answer the two questions under the numbers. They ask what went wrong and what you
   liked. **Send** posts them with this Exit; **Skip** leaves the summary as it is.

The Company is over. It goes into your history, with its board, its Stack, and its payout.

## Spend your Legacy

1. Select **Legacy shop** on the summary. The shop lists each Perk with its level, its effect, and
   its price in Legacy.
2. Select one Perk that you can afford. Its level goes up by one.
3. Select **Back**. The main menu opens.

Perks last for every future Company. For example, **Angel network** doubles your starting Cash,
and **Institutional knowledge** keeps one Research item through an Exit. See
[Perks](/prestige#perks) for the full list.

If you prefer to save your Legacy, skip the purchase. Legacy also unlocks new
[Ventures](/prestige#ventures).

## Found your next Company

1. On the main menu, select **Start a company**. The New company screen opens.
2. Type a name in **Company name** and select a logo. Each Company keeps its own name and logo.
3. Read the **Venture** list. A Venture sets the kind of company: its request mix, its demand
   shape, and its rules. Your first Venture is selected.
4. Read the **Mutators** list. Mutators make a Company harder for more Legacy. Your first Exit opens four
   of them: Legacy monolith, One region forever, Shared tenancy, and Tight budget. Achievements open
   the rest, so those still show a lock.
5. Select **Found**. Its row shows the starting Era and the Legacy multiplier.

The new Company starts over: new Cash, new users, no Research, and a new board. Your Legacy, your
Perks, and your history stay. The Company you exited closes: its file leaves the **Companies** list,
and you cannot continue it. Its history row and Legacy stay with you.

Your next Company also shows every control from the start, and it adds **Playbooks**. Press **P** to
open them. A Playbook saves part of a Design, so you can place a known-good Garage setup in one
action.

## What you learned

- Exits open in Series A, and the Exit button shows the Legacy you can earn.
- The payout is Equity times valuation times the Exit's multiplier.
- Legacy buys Perks that last for every future Company.
- A new Company starts over, but faster, with Perks and Playbooks.
