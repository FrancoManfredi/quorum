Quorum is free and has no ads. One developer builds most of it, with help from
[contributors](https://github.com/elianiva/quorum/graphs/contributors). The people on this page
chipped in on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/elianiva), and their support pays for servers and for the
hours that go into the next Era. Thank you.

## Thank you

The list is empty for now, so the first name here could be yours.

## Join them

Send a one-off tip or a monthly amount on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/elianiva). Leave your name in
the message if you want it on this page; leave it out and you stay anonymous.
